# Research Questions

## Main Research Question

How can an intelligent web-based system effectively match users with suitable job opportunities based on their education, work experience, and technical skills?

## Sub-Research Questions

### RQ1
What factors should be considered when matching a user's profile with the requirements of a job position?

### RQ2
How can the compatibility between a user's skills and a job's required skills be calculated?

### RQ3
How can a system identify the skill gaps between a user's current skills and the requirements of a desired job?

### RQ4
How can job opportunities be ranked and recommended based on the compatibility between user profiles and job requirements?

### RQ5
How can the effectiveness and quality of the proposed job recommendation system be evaluated?

### RQ6
To what extent can natural language processing or machine learning techniques improve job matching and recommendation?
